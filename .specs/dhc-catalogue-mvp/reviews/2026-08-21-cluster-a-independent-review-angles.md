# PR #97: angle reports from the `code-review` skill run, 2026-08-21

The independent reviewer launched the `code-review` skill against `spec-cluster-a`.
Session usage limits cut the skill's runs short before it consolidated a report; four of
its angle agents completed and delivered their findings to the coordinating session, not
to the reviewer, which therefore folded in only the "removed behaviour" angle it found on
disk. The four completed reports are preserved here verbatim (JSON as returned), so the
evidence the reviewer did not see is not lost. Overlap with the reviewer's numbered
findings is substantial (the published/frozen gap, the unsigned re-point, the switch
contradictions, "platform manifest", the flow-diagram ordering); the distinct points are
marked in the index below.

## Index of points not already in the independent review

- Altitude: publish-if-changed compensates for a non-reproducible build; BuildKit
  reproducibility (`SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH`, `rewrite-timestamp`, provenance `reproducible`)
  was never weighed or measured against the `dhi.io/build` frontend.
- Altitude: Req 2.12's `under_investigation` statements derive from an unpersisted input
  (the release-time scan report), so the attested document is not reproducible from
  source; persist the report or a committed record; Req 6.32 records omissions, nothing
  records additions.
- Altitude: "declared" switches (fail-closed, publish policy, schedule, verification
  inputs) have no declared location; cluster B's `triage/policy.yaml` is the natural
  home, with workflows, the Kyverno rendering and the README snippet rendered from it.
- Altitude: scan each platform manifest by its own digest instead of `--platform` plus
  `--image-src remote`; self-describing reports, no silent downgrade, RepoDigest equals
  the manifest digest the compiler stamps.
- Altitude: Req 2.21 belongs to one "daily invariants" mechanism together with the
  ruleset-drift, private-reporting, revoked-digest and checkpoint-3 checks.
- Simplification: Req 6.35 is implied by Req 6.1 (the reviewer disagrees and keeps it);
  the coverage predicate is copied three times and cluster B will change it, so define
  "uncovered finding" once; Req 2.19, 2.20 and 2.22 are three criteria for one
  invariant; Decision 6's Decision paragraph transcribes the criteria instead of stating
  the choice.
- EARS pitfalls: Req 2.24's "its daily checks" is a third name for the daily run; Req 2.9
  gates tagging on "those attestations" and not on the signature; Req 2.20 is an
  event-shaped response under a bare WHILE.
